What we usually find

Most local businesses have customers. They just don't own the path to them.

A busy Instagram, word of mouth, a promoter who fills the room. Then a customer searches for you, and the trail goes cold.

📍

Not on the map

No Google, Apple Maps or Waze listing, or one with the wrong name, hours or phone.

🔗

No home base

No website, and no single link that holds your menu or services, hours, booking and directions.

💬

Booking lives in DMs

Reservations and quotes get lost in messages. No deposit, no confirmation, no follow-up.

⭐

Few or no reviews

Reviews drive map ranking and trust. Without a simple way to ask for them, they don't come in.

📇

No customer list

Customers walk out without leaving a phone number or email, so there's no way to bring them back.

⏱️

Everything is manual

Answering the same questions, confirming bookings, chasing reviews. AI and automation can take that work off your plate.

FAQ

Questions owners ask

Is the audit really free?

Yes. No payment info, no contract. If the audit shows you don't need us, we'll tell you.

Do you need my passwords?

No. You add us as a manager or partner on Google, Meta and your payment processor, and you can remove us any time. Accounts stay in your name.

Who owns the website, listings and customer list?

You do. If you ever stop working with us, we hand everything over, including a copy of the site.

How long does it take?

The audit takes up to 3 business days. A full foundation build usually takes 3 to 4 weeks. Google sets its own timing for verifying listings, which can add a few days.

Can you run ads for any business?

Not always. Meta and Google restrict ads for some categories, like tobacco and hookah, cannabis, firearms and some health services. The audit tells you what's allowed for your business and what we'd do instead.

What does "AI and automation" actually mean here?

Practical things: auto-replies that send your booking link, automatic confirmations and reminders, drafted review replies you approve, and follow-up messages to customers who opted in. Nothing posts or sends without rules you've signed off on.
