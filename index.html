06 Questions

What owners ask us first.

Is the audit really free?

Yes. No payment details, no contract. If it shows you don't need us, we'll tell you.

Do you need my passwords?

No. You add us as a manager or partner on Google, Meta and your payment processor, and you can remove us at any time. The accounts stay in your name.

Who owns the website, listings and customer list?

You do. If you ever stop working with us, we hand everything over, including a copy of the site.

How long does it take?

The audit takes up to 3 business days. A full build usually takes 3 to 4 weeks. Google sets its own timing for verifying listings, which can add a few days.

Can you run ads for any business?

Not always. Meta and Google restrict some categories, like tobacco and hookah, cannabis, firearms and some health services. The audit tells you what's allowed for your business and what we'd do instead.

What does "AI and automation" mean here?

Practical things: replies that send your booking link, confirmations and reminders, drafted review replies you approve, and follow-ups to customers who opted in. Nothing sends without rules you've signed off on.
